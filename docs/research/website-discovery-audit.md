# SeedPower Foundation - Website Discovery and Content Audit Report

**Date of Audit:** November 17, 2026  
**Audit Scope:** Comprehensive analysis of existing website at https://seedpowerfoundation.org/  
**Reference Project:** https://www.africacsid.org/  
**Status:** DISCOVERY PHASE - No implementation yet

---

## Executive Summary

The existing SeedPower Foundation website is minimal and appears to be a single-page or very limited multi-page site. Only **5 accessible pages** were discovered during systematic exploration. The site contains important organizational information, mission details, programs, and fundraising information, but is severely limited in scope, navigation, and content organization compared to the reference project (Africa CSID).

**Key Finding:** The current website lacks comprehensive information architecture. Many expected pages do not exist (About Us expanded, Leadership bios, individual program pages, gallery, blog, news section, etc.). This audit captures all existing accessible content and identifies significant gaps that should be addressed in the new website design.
