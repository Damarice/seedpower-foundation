SeedPower Gala Invitation

Dear Seedpower Friends,

We are honored to welcome you to our second SeedPower Community Indaba Gala 2026 and are deeply grateful for your continued partnership with SeedPower Foundation. Our Gala will be held on Tuesday, February 24, 2026, at the ORU – Global Learning Center, located at 7777 S. Lewis (formerly the Baby Mabee). Doors will open at 5:00 to shop the Market and begin bidding on silent auction items. All proceeds raised will support our ongoing work serving vulnerable communities in Kenya, East Africa, and beyond.

As we reflect on the past year, our hearts are filled with gratitude for God’s faithfulness. At last year’s event we prayerfully set critical financial goals for the growth and sustainability of our schools. Through God’s provision and your generosity, those goals were not only met but exceeded! We are continually amazed by how God works through faithful and willing communities.

Because of your support, we have seen meaningful and encouraging progress at both of our school campuses. These positive changes are creating stronger environments where students can thrive academically, spiritually, and emotionally.

Our mission is to empower Kenya’s vulnerable communities through education, resources, and skills for long-term self-reliance. Indaba is a time to gather, celebrate what God has done, and look forward to the work ahead—together. Sponsorship, exhibitor, and ticket opportunities are available, and we hope you will join us.

Thank you for your prayers, generosity, and continued support.

With sincere gratitude and hope,

Blessings, 

The Seedpower Advisory Board

SeedPower Foundation

Jim Grinnell, Laura Grinnell, Beldon Okeyo, Peter Opinya, Linda Steed, Jean Winfrey, Keith Winfrey
